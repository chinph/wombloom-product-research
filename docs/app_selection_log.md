## 5. Segmentation rules

- **Direct competitor**: pregnancy-focused (name, or first 400 characters of description) AND mentions journal/diary/memories/keepsake/scrapbook in the name or first 400 characters of the description.
- **Category leader**: pregnancy in the app name AND at least 20,000 ratings.
- **Adjacent**: not pregnancy-named, mentions baby/newborn/postpartum AND feeding/sleep/diaper/growth/tracking.
- **Pregnancy other**: pregnancy-focused apps that fit none of the above.

### Notable exclusions
- **Cycle and period trackers** (Flo, Clue, Glow Eve, Ovia Cycle's siblings): different product, removed by an explicit filter.
- **Off-topic hits** that mention pregnancy or babies: prenatal yoga, massage, baby names, face generators, general fitness. They are in the "Pregnancy other" segment and not shortlisted.
- **Tiny pregnancy journals** (BabyBound, Little Sprout, Bun, Dear Baby and similar, under 100 ratings): the most direct competitors by concept but too few reviews to analyze.

### Findings worth noting
- The apps closest to Wombloom's concept have very few ratings. The "direct competitor" group had to relax the 1,000-rating rule, and that is documented here.
- Wombloom is not yet released, so it is not part of the competitor set. This research is pre-launch: it informs what to build.
